How to play

  • Two players, Red and Yellow, take turns dropping one disc into any of the 7 columns.
  • A disc falls to the lowest empty spot in the column you pick.
  • Be the first to line up four of your own discs to win.
  • If the board fills up with no line of four, the game is a draw.
